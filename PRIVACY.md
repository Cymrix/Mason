# Privacy Policy for Mason 2D Level Editor
**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

Mason 2D Level Editor ("Mason", "we", "our") respects your privacy.

## 1. Overview
Mason is a client-side web application. All project data, biome configurations, custom tilemaps, and sprite sheets are stored locally in your browser's `localStorage` / `IndexedDB` unless you choose to connect Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive.

## 2. Google User Data Policy Compliance
Mason requests access to the restricted `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file` scope when you connect Google Drive. This permission allows Mason to save, open, and sync `.mason` level files created by or explicitly opened with Mason inside your Google Drive.

- Mason does **not** store, harvest, sell, or share your personal data or Google Drive files on any third-party external server.
- OAuth tokens reside exclusively in your browser session.
- Mason **cannot** read, modify, or delete any unassociated files in your Google Drive.

## 3. Microsoft OneDrive
Mason uses Microsoft Graph API with `Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder` scope to save level files inside a dedicated `MasonMapEditor` folder in your OneDrive.
